CRAFT / PRODUCTION TECHNIQUES

Natural material. Precise execution.

From digital layout to shaped, carved, and patterned finishes — made for your project.

01 Planning & measurement

Marble scanning & digital dry-lay

Slabs are scanned and digitally arranged against your floor plan, so cutting and veining are planned before any stone is cut.

Cutting & polishing to site measurements

Slabs are cut to the measured dimensions of the site and polished to the required finish.

02 Lamination onto substrates

Honeycomb panel

Lightweight panels for facades. Also used for sinks so the piece ships rigid as one unit.

Sintered stone

Placed under marble floors to block rising moisture and protect light marble from rust stains and ground-up humidity.

Polycarbonate

Used with backlighting to illuminate more marble types while keeping the installation light and easy to fit.

03 Shaping & carving

Curved marble features

Single or multiple curves, carved from a solid block or assembled from sections and polished into a continuous surface.

Artwork & relief carving

Features and artwork carved from thick marble, from shallow relief to deeper bas-relief sculpture.

04 Pattern & inlay work

Marble marquetry (stone inlay)

Patterns inlaid into tabletops or floors. Combine different marbles in a custom pattern or artwork.

Marble mosaic

Made to order from different marbles and patterns, or selected from our existing mosaic range, slab types, and colors.
